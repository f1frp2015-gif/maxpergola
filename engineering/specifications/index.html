Aluminum Specifications & BOM

These are the visible commercial design inputs for product comparison and quote review. The approved shop drawing, material certificate, finish sample, packing list, and engineering documents control the final order.

6063-T6 is the order design basis. Max Pergola requires the actual alloy and temper to be confirmed by mill certificate in the order documentation. Profile dimensions below are taken from the current product profile drawing; they do not establish load capacity by themselves.

Documents to Confirm Before Production

Material and finish

Alloy/temper certificate, extrusion profile revision, powder-coat system, color, gloss, surface acceptance, and applicable coating test evidence.

Engineering scope

Applicable code, site loads, risk category, exposure, topography, spans, connections, anchors, foundation assumptions, drainage, and required professional seal.

Commercial scope

SKU, dimensions, accessories, electrical components, packing list, spare parts, warranty, DDP boundary, unloading, schedule, and exclusions.
