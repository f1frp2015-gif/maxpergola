Partner FAQ

Know the commercial terms before you commit.

Each approval is documented. A headline percentage never replaces the written agreement or quote.

Who can apply to the pergola partner program?

Dealers, fabricators, installers, contractors, builders, design-build firms, landscape professionals, and qualified commercial buyers may apply. Approval depends on business fit, service capability, territory, and expected volume.

How is my partner discount selected?

The account tier reflects your role, order volume, display or inventory commitment, project responsibilities, and local service capability. The maximum percentages shown on this page are modeled ceilings, not automatic discounts for every order.

Do I need to hold inventory?

No. Installers and builders can register individual projects. Dealer and fabricator programs may include display units, stocking plans, or consolidated orders when those routes make commercial sense.

What is excluded from the product discount?

Unless a written quote says otherwise, installation labor, foundations, anchors, electrical work, permits, taxes, unloading, site work, and project-specific freight are separate from the product price.
