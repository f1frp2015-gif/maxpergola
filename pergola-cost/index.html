Pergola Cost Breakdown: Seven Budget Buckets

Ask every seller and installer to assign each line item to included, excluded, allowance, or owner-supplied.

01

Pergola kit

Frame, roof package, controls, finish, standard or custom dimensions, connection hardware, documents, and selected accessories.

02

Freight and unloading

Ship-from point, DDP destination, jobsite access, crate handling, lift equipment, receiving labor, storage, and damage inspection.

03

Site and foundation

Demolition, grading, drainage, concrete, footings, slab verification, anchors, restoration, and site-specific engineering.

04

Assembly labor

Crew mobilization, frame, louvers, drainage, accessories, alignment, torque checks, cleanup, testing, and return visits.

05

Electrical work

Circuits, disconnects, grounding, bonding, GFCI protection, motors, controls, lighting, outlets, permits, and inspections.

06

Permits and review

Application fees, drawings, engineering review, plan revisions, inspections, and any local professional services.

07

Taxes and contingency

Applicable sales or use tax plus an identified allowance for concealed site conditions, repairs, or approved scope changes.

Size and layout

Larger Is Not the Only Cost Variable

Footprint changes material and freight, but attachment, site access, foundations, accessories, and custom coordination can matter just as much.

  • Measure furniture plus chair pullback and walkways
  • Record doors, eaves, gutters, pools, grills, and utilities
  • Choose freestanding or attached before requesting bids
  • Use standard sizes when they fit the intended activity
  • Request custom dimensions only after the site is measured

Installation cost

Fast Frame Assembly Does Not Erase Site Work

The prepared frame is designed for a typical 45-minute assembly by an experienced two- to three-person crew after the site and anchors are ready. Budget the complete sequence.

Product assembly

Unloading, staging, frame, louvers, drainage, controls, accessories, alignment, and testing.

Closeout

Worldwide factory-direct delivery

A DDP Quote Still Needs Jobsite Detail

Orders can be quoted factory-direct from Chongqing under Delivered Duty Paid terms to supported destinations worldwide. The written quote must still identify the exact destination, included duties and taxes, unloading responsibility, access requirements, route restrictions, and exclusions.

  • Provide the delivery country, postal code or city, and complete receiving address
  • Describe truck access, gates, grades, and turning limits
  • Confirm who provides unloading labor and equipment
  • Plan secure storage and prompt carton inspection
  • Use the written quote as the controlling shipping scope

Regional inventory may supplement the factory route for confirmed standard stock. Availability is not guaranteed; the order confirmation identifies the actual ship-from point and route.

Freight, unloading, storage, and final placement are separate decisions.

Bid comparison

Normalize Every Quote Before Choosing a Price

A low number may simply stop at the factory gate, slab edge, or frame assembly. Put competing proposals into the same seven cost buckets before comparing totals.

Separate fixed prices from allowances

Mark each amount as fixed, estimated, allowance, unit rate, optional, excluded, or owner-supplied. Ask what changes the price, how long it remains valid, which taxes apply, and how approved change orders are documented. A foundation allowance without excavation depth, reinforcement, concrete, anchors, disposal, and restoration is not a complete foundation price.

Record quantities and product identity for motors, controls, lights, screens, glass, heaters, outlets, spare parts, and finish upgrades. Confirm whether freight reaches the curb, an accessible unloading point, secure storage, or the final assembly area.

Compare responsibility and schedule

Name the party responsible for measurements, permits, engineering, utility marking, foundations, anchors, unloading, frame assembly, roof work, electrical connections, drainage discharge, testing, inspection, cleanup, and warranty service. Gaps between trades often become owner costs later.

Build a schedule around approvals, production, transit, concrete cure, installer availability, electrical work, and inspections rather than the frame-assembly estimate alone. Include a clearly identified contingency for concealed conditions or owner-approved scope changes instead of hiding risk inside an unexplained lump sum.

Contingency planning

Keep Known Scope Out of the Contingency

A contingency is for uncertainty, not for work that everyone already knows the project needs.

Price visible requirements directly

Put permits, required drawings, freight, unloading, concrete, anchors, electrical circuits, finish repairs, drainage discharge, equipment rental, and inspection fees into their correct budget buckets. Leaving predictable work inside a percentage allowance makes bids difficult to compare and increases the chance of a surprise later.

Describe the risks behind the allowance

Existing-slab condition, concealed utilities, demolition quantity, restricted access, soil uncertainty, or required local revisions may justify a separate reserve. Record who can authorize its use, what evidence is required, how unused funds are treated, and whether schedule impact is included. Revisit the allowance as site information and approvals become more complete.

Motorized pergola cost FAQ

Questions Before You Compare Prices

How much does a motorized pergola cost?

Max Pergola's published starting kit prices are $5,034 for the Pro 10×10 and $7,239 for the Max 10×10 as reviewed August 24, 2026. Installed cost also depends on delivery, unloading, foundations, anchors, labor, electrical work, accessories, permits, taxes, and inspections.

What comes in the kit price?

The approved bill of materials and written quote define the shipment. A typical kit may include the aluminum frame, roof components, connection hardware, and assembly documents. Foundations, anchors, tools, electrical work, installation, and accessories may be separate.

Does a motorized pergola cost more than a manual model?

A motorized roof adds drives, controls, wiring preparation, field electrical work, and future service considerations. The final difference depends on the package, footprint, lighting, and other accessories.

Is installation included?

Do not assume it is included. The written quote identifies product, freight, unloading responsibility, available support, and exclusions. Obtain separate local bids for foundations, labor, electrical work, permits, and taxes when necessary.
