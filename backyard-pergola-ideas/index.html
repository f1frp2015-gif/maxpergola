Backyard pergola ideas

Build a backyard around the moments you want more of.

The best pergola idea begins with a use: dinner without glare, coffee in a quiet corner or a poolside kitchen that stays comfortable at noon.

Layout rules that help

Make the beautiful idea work in real weather.

Orientation should respond to more than the view. Track sun, prevailing weather, existing roof runoff and where integrated post drains can discharge without creating a new problem.

  • Map morning and afternoon sun across the seasons
  • Keep grills, fire features and pools within required clearances
  • Preserve comfortable circulation around furniture
  • Route pergola drainage away from foundations and walkways
  • Choose screened sides based on wind and low-angle sun
