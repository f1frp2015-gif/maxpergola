From concept to reviewed scope

How the Pergola Configurator Supports a Better Quote

The builder records all decisions that affect product scope without presenting a concept image as an approved drawing for the project.

01

Comparable dimensions

Explore 8–24 ft widths, 8–18 ft louver spans, and 7–10 ft clear heights before production geometry is confirmed. The result is a consistent comparison across common residential and commercial footprints.

02

Connected outdoor living

Keep smart controls, weather response, lighting, comfort accessories, and all four side systems in one configuration. This prevents accessory scope from being split across emails and follow-up notes.

03

Reviewable handoff

A shareable URL and configuration code preserve every choice for pricing, engineering, availability, and delivery review. Project teams can review the same selection set without rebuilding it manually.

Use the preview to compare, not to measure

The real-time 3D view compares proportions, finishes, roof position, supports, accessories, and side systems. It is not a structural model, color sample, permit document, or installation drawing. Attached structures still require wall, flashing, drainage, connection, and foundation review.

Confirm the complete project around the kit

Final review covers site wind and snow criteria, foundations, anchors, electrical scope, clearances, drainage, delivery, installation, permits, pricing, and exclusions. Use the wind, snow and span calculator for early screening; the accepted quote and approved documents control.

Configuration is not approval. Final outside dimensions, member layout, controls, finish, accessories, structural criteria, electrical scope, lead time, delivery terms, and price must be confirmed in writing for the specific project.
