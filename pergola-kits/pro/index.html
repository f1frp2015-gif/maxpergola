Motorized Pergola: Control Shade From the Couch

A motorized pergola makes frequent roof adjustments easier, but reliable operation begins with documented power, controls, drainage, and service access.

Remote operation for everyday changes

Use the quoted control interface to open the louvers for sky and ventilation, angle them for directional shade, or move them to the documented closed position for ordinary rain management. The base Pro description includes a motorized drive, control box, and specified handheld control or remote. It does not promise a particular radio protocol, wall switch, phone app, voice assistant, wind sensor, or home-automation platform unless that item appears in the accepted quote.

Keep a clear view of the roof while it moves. Stop operation when snow, ice, branches, tools, packaging, or another obstruction could interfere with the blades. Owners should know where the disconnect or isolation point is, how to identify unusual movement, and whom to contact before resetting or forcing a stalled system.

Confirm electrical data before rough-in

Motor voltage, frequency, rated input, current, ingress protection, control type, conductor requirements, and the quantity of drives can vary by approved configuration. Those values belong on the selected motor nameplate, wiring diagram, quote, and shop drawing—not in a generic marketing claim. Give that documentation to the project electrician before branch-circuit sizing or conduit placement.

The field electrical scope may include a dedicated or shared circuit, disconnecting means, grounding or bonding, wet-location boxes and fittings, surge protection, control wiring, and inspection. Applicable requirements depend on the equipment, site, and local authority. Electrical work and commissioning should be completed by a qualified person where required.

Pro planning sequence

Plan Controls, Lighting, and Service Access Together

Before production

Confirm the footprint, roof orientation, motor location, control interface, power-entry point, LED preparation, drain routes, nearby walls, and desired accessories. Record whether a phone app or smart-home connection is a requirement so compatibility can be accepted or excluded in writing.

Before electrical rough-in

Release work from the approved wiring information rather than an early sales image. Coordinate conduit and driver locations with gutters, post drains, moving louvers, screens, glass, heaters, outlets, and required service clearances.

At commissioning

Operate the full roof range, check for binding, verify controls and limits, inspect cable protection, and test drainage. Record model and serial information, breaker or disconnect location, warranty documents, and the responsible service contact for the owner.

Pro FAQ

Questions About a Motorized Pergola

Does the Pro motorized pergola include a remote?

The package scope lists a handheld control or remote specified in the accepted quote. Confirm the exact control model, quantity, operating method, and any wall-control requirement in the order documents.

Can I control the pergola with an app?

App, Wi-Fi, voice-assistant, and smart-home compatibility are not assumed in the base description. Request the desired platform before production so the control hardware, network requirements, commissioning, support, and exclusions can be verified.

What voltage and power does the roof motor use?

Use the approved motor nameplate and wiring diagram for the selected configuration. Do not size the branch circuit from a generic webpage value. The electrician should confirm voltage, frequency, current, protection, conductor, disconnect, and local inspection requirements before rough-in.

What happens during a power outage?

The powered roof will not operate normally without its required electrical supply. Ask whether the selected drive has a documented manual or backup procedure; do not improvise one. Keep the roof and drainage maintained and follow the supplied operating instructions.
