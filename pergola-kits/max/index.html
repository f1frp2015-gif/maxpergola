Premium control planning

Coordinate the Roof, Lighting, and Smart-Home Brief

Max is designed for a more coordinated outdoor room. The value is in resolving component relationships before extrusion, wiring routes, accessory mounts, and service access are locked.

Define the experience first

List the scenes the space should support: open-air daytime dining, angled afternoon shade, ordinary-rain cover, low-glare evening light, privacy for a spa, screened seating, or power near an outdoor kitchen. That brief determines which accessories need physical preparation and which items require separate trades or controls.

Louver-mounted lighting creates a different visual effect from perimeter lighting. It can emphasize the roof rhythm and distribute light above furniture, but fixture placement, cable protection, drivers, dimming method, color temperature, service access, and electrical listing still need written confirmation. Images communicate an atmosphere; the accepted quote defines what ships.

Treat automation as a compatibility requirement

The Max package includes a specified motorized control interface, not a blanket promise of compatibility with every phone app, hub, voice assistant, building-management system, sensor, or wireless standard. Name the required platform and desired behaviors before production. The supplier can then confirm an approved interface, identify third-party work, or clearly exclude the request.

Likewise, a large or multi-zone layout may use one or more roof modules and drives. Drive quantity is determined by the approved configuration; “dual motor” is not automatically included simply because the package is called Max. The shop drawing, BOM, motor schedule, and wiring diagram must agree before rough-in.

Size and engineering

Plan the Largest Footprint From Verified Loads and Spans

Start with the usable outdoor room

Measure the furniture at full use, circulation paths, cooking clearances, doors, eaves, pool edges, utilities, and views. A bigger roof is not automatically a better layout if posts interrupt movement or the structure crowds the house.

Separate module size from unsupported span

Overall width and depth do not by themselves state the distance a beam can span. Column placement, roof direction, internal framing, connections, bracing, and accessories change the structural model. Record the longest unsupported member for review.

Do not infer a maximum from a sales table

No universal maximum span is claimed on this page. Wind, snow, exposure, topography, risk category, deflection criteria, section properties, anchors, substrate, foundation, and local approval govern the project-specific result. Use the calculator to organize inputs, not to certify capacity.

Build an accessory matrix before approval. For every light, screen, glass panel, privacy slat, heater, outlet, sensor, and control, record the supplier, exact model, quantity, mounting location, power, route, control zone, service clearance, field trade, and whether the item ships with the pergola. Also define the night-use priorities in plain language: low-glare conversation, brighter cooking and cleanup, safe paths, television viewing, or architectural roof emphasis. Those scenes help the lighting and controls team place equipment intentionally instead of treating the premium package as a generic collection of accessories.

Max FAQ

Questions About the Max Package

Does Max include dual motors?

Not automatically. The number of roof modules and drives depends on the approved size, layout, loads, control zones, and selected equipment. Confirm the motor schedule, wiring diagram, and BOM for the quoted configuration.

Will the motorized louvered pergola work with my smart home?

Compatibility must be checked against the named platform, interface, region, network, and desired automations. App, hub, voice, and sensor functions are included only when the accepted quote and control documentation list them.

Are lights, screens, glass, and heaters included?

Only itemized products are included. Factory “preparation” may mean mounting, routing, or coordination provisions rather than the fixture, driver, panel, heater, outlet, wiring, trade labor, or commissioning itself.

What is the maximum span?

There is no single website maximum that applies to every site. The approved configuration must be checked for actual wind, snow, geometry, section properties, connections, anchors, substrate, foundation, accessories, and local requirements.

How should I plan the night scene?

Identify task, ambient, and path lighting separately. Record fixture locations, brightness and dimming goals, color temperature, control zones, glare-sensitive seating, nearby neighbors, electrical routes, and service access before the shop drawing is approved.
