Kit anatomy

The details that make a kit feel complete.

A well-planned pergola kit is more than four posts and a roof. It coordinates shade, water, wiring and finish so each decision supports the next.

  • Adjustable louver roof for sun and rain control
  • Integrated drainage channels and post outlets
  • Prepared pathways for compatible LED lighting
  • Powder-coated aluminum frame and concealed hardware
  • Labeled, protected export packaging

How to choose the right pergola kit

1. Plan the usable zone

Measure the table, grill, sofa or spa first. Add circulation space, chair pull-back room and comfortable distance from hot surfaces. The most useful pergola covers how people move, not only where the furniture sits today.

2. Verify the mounting surface

A sound concrete slab, engineered footings or another approved substrate must transfer loads safely. Pavers alone are generally a finish surface—not a structural foundation. Confirm the anchoring plan locally.

3. Define weather criteria

Wind exposure, snow, rainfall, freezing, coastal conditions and nearby roof runoff all affect configuration and upkeep. Provide the project location early so the specification can be reviewed against applicable needs.

4. Choose options before production

Motorized louvers, LEDs, screens and glass can change wiring and profile preparation. Planning these at the quote stage is cleaner than retrofitting after delivery.
