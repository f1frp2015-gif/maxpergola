Pergola Kits: How to Choose the Right One

1. Measure the usable area

Lay out the table, grill, sectional, or spa first. Add chair pullback, walkways, and required clearances from cooking and fire equipment. Size the roof for the way people will move through the space.

2. Verify the foundation

The post bases must anchor to a structural concrete slab, engineered footings, or another approved substrate. Pavers are typically a finish surface, not a foundation. Have the footing and anchor design confirmed for the site.

3. Identify the design conditions

Wind exposure, snow load, rainfall, freeze-thaw conditions, coastal exposure, and existing roof runoff can affect the structure and drainage plan. Provide the jobsite address early so the applicable design criteria can be reviewed.

4. Select accessories before production

Motors, LEDs, retractable screens, glass panels, heaters, and outlets may require dedicated wiring paths or mounting preparation. Include them in the quote instead of assuming they can be added later.
